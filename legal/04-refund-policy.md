## 1. Your legal right of withdrawal, and the choice you make at checkout

**If you are a consumer in the EU or UK**, you normally have 14 days to withdraw
from a distance purchase for any reason. Digital content is a special case: you
lose that right *only if* you expressly ask for delivery to begin immediately
**and** acknowledge that you are giving the right up. (EU Consumer Rights
Directive 2011/83/EU, Article 16(m).)

So, when you buy, you will be asked to confirm:

> *"I want my licence key sent immediately, and I understand that I lose my
> 14-day right of withdrawal once it is sent."*

**If you tick it**, we send your key straight away and the statutory 14-day right
ends at that moment. Our own 30-day promise below still applies — it is more
generous than the law requires.

**If you do not tick it**, we wait 14 days before sending your key, and your
statutory right is untouched for the whole period.

Either way you keep the 14-day free trial, which is why almost nobody needs
this: you can evaluate the entire app before any money moves.

**Nothing here removes a statutory right.** Where this policy and the law
disagree, the law wins.

## 2. Our own promise: 30 days

Beyond the statutory position, we refund any purchase within **30 days of
payment** if you ask. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you bought with,
say you want a refund, and you do not have to give a reason.

We aim to process within 5 working days, by the route you paid.

## 3. Cases we refund without question, at any time

These are our fault, not yours:

- **You bought it and it will not launch on your Mac.** ARMA Island needs an
  **Apple Silicon** Mac (M1 or later) and **macOS 14.0+**. It is arm64-only and
  will not start on an Intel Mac at all. If you missed that, it is our job to
  have made it clearer.
- **Your licence key never arrived or does not work**, and we cannot fix it.
- **The app is materially not what this site described.**

## 4. When we may say no

- Beyond 30 days, where no statutory right applies and nothing in section 3 fits.
- Repeated refunds by the same buyer — one refund is a mistake, a pattern is
  something else.
- Where a key was shared publicly.

If we say no, we will say why.

## 5. What happens to your licence

A refunded licence key stops being valid. Please delete the app. Because the app
verifies keys offline and never contacts us, we have no way to switch your copy
off remotely — this is on your honour, and it is a consequence of the same
design choice that means we cannot track you.

## 6. How a refund actually reaches you

There is no card checkout and no payment processor — you paid us directly, so
there is no automatic reversal to trigger. We refund by the same route the money
arrived, by hand, which is why we ask for a couple of days rather than promising
an instant credit.

It also means **there is no chargeback button to press**. If something goes
wrong, email is not the polite first step, it is the only step. We have never
refused a reasonable refund.
