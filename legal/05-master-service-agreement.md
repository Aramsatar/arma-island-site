**Status: not needed for the $15 consumer sale. Needed the first time a company
buys in bulk or asks for terms.**

**Last updated:** [DATE]

## When to use this instead of the standard Terms

The Terms of Sale and Licence cover an individual buying one licence for $15.
Use this MSA when a **company** is the customer and any of these is true: they
are buying multiple seats, they want an invoice and payment terms, they want
support commitments, or their procurement sends you a vendor agreement.

If none of that applies, do not send this — a two-page MSA in front of a $15
purchase kills the sale and creates obligations you did not need.

## 1. Structure

This MSA sets the framework. Each purchase is an **Order** specifying seats,
price, and term. Where an Order and this MSA conflict, the Order wins for that
purchase.

## 2. Licence

The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence for
the number of seats in the Order, for use by its personnel on Macs it owns or
controls. Seats may be reassigned between individuals; they may not be shared
concurrently.

Licence keys are bound to an email address and verified offline. The software
performs no remote activation check, so **seat counts rely on the Customer's own
administration**, not on enforcement.

## 3. Delivery and system requirements

Delivered by download plus licence keys by email. The Customer is responsible for
checking, before purchase, that its fleet meets the requirements:

- **Apple Silicon only** (M1 or later) — the software will not launch on Intel.
- **macOS 14.0 or later.**

The Supplier will confirm compatibility in writing on request. A fleet that does
not meet these requirements is grounds for a full refund under clause 9.

## 4. What the software does on an endpoint

Material for any security review, and set out in full in the Privacy Policy. In
summary the software: reads the clipboard and stores a local history; reads
coding-agent session files including prompt text and executed commands; modifies
`~/.claude/settings.json`; and sends Apple Events to media and terminal
applications, which for Chrome exposes window and tab titles to it.

It has **no network entitlement**, no server, no account and no telemetry, so
none of this leaves the endpoint.

The Customer is responsible for deciding whether that behaviour is acceptable
under its own policies, and for informing its personnel.

auto-updater. The Supplier does not commit to a release schedule or to specific

**Dependency risk, stated plainly:** the now-playing feature uses a private
macOS interface that Apple may change or remove without notice. The Supplier
will make reasonable efforts to adapt and does not warrant continued
availability of that feature.

## 9. Warranties and remedy

The Supplier warrants that the software will perform materially as documented
for **90 days** from delivery. The sole remedy for breach is: the Supplier fixes
it, or refunds the fees for the affected seats. Otherwise the software is
provided **as is**.

## 10. Liability

Neither party excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by
negligence, fraud, or anything else that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Subject to that, **each party's total aggregate liability is limited to the fees
paid under the relevant Order in the 12 months before the claim**, and neither
party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, lost profits, or lost data.

*[Note to the Supplier: with no company formed, this cap is the only thing

## 11. Indemnity

The Supplier will defend the Customer against a third-party claim that the
software infringes an IP right, and pay damages finally awarded, provided the
Customer notifies promptly, gives control of the defence, and cooperates. The
Supplier may modify the software, obtain a licence, or refund the fees and
terminate. This does not apply to modified or misused copies.

## 12. Term and termination

Runs until terminated. Either party may terminate for material breach not cured
within **30 days** of notice, or immediately on the other's insolvency.
Perpetual licences already paid for survive termination; support and updates
stop.
