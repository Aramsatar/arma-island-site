Changelog

Every release, including the honest parts. Your copy shows its version in the island's settings, under Licence.

1.0.5

2 August 2026

You can reach us from inside the app. A Help row in settings: "Report a problem" opens a draft in your own mail client with your version, Mac and permission state filled in — nothing is sent until you send it, because the app still makes no network connections. "Copy diagnostics" for when you're already talking to us.

Grant Accessibility from the panel. Before, the only way to grant the media-keys permission was pressing a transport button while music played in Chrome — and macOS shows that dialog exactly once. Now a "Media keys" row asks properly, opens System Settings if needed, and the panel notices when you've granted it instead of staying dimmed.

The Buy button now points at armaisland.app — this site, at its real address.

1.0.4

2 August 2026

Idle CPU from 12.8% of a core to 0.7%. The island kept animating when nothing was happening; now it genuinely rests. If your fans had opinions about ARMA Island, this is the release that quiets them.

Running the binary with --help now answers instead of launching the app.

1.0.2

1 August 2026

The media buttons stopped pretending. Controlling Chrome or YouTube Music needs macOS Accessibility permission, and macOS drops the keypress silently without it — so the buttons looked alive and did nothing. Now the app checks first, says exactly what is missing, and asks for it. Spotify and Apple Music always worked and still need nothing.

1.0.1

1 August 2026

The album-art fetch is gone. Our privacy page said the app holds no network connections; the app was fetching cover art from a CDN. We fixed the app, not the wording — the code that could reach the network was removed entirely. The app now makes no network connections at all.

1.0.0

July 2026

First release. The island, the face, seven moods, agent session cards, media controls, a 14-day trial and an offline licence.
